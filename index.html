1. The Industry Challenge

Repair is rational, but replacement still feels easier and more desirable.

Families often discard jackets, backpacks, school gear, and everyday clothing because small damage feels inconvenient, unattractive, or not worth the time to fix.

Risk

Throwaway habits weaken sustainable brand behavior and normalize short product life.

Opportunity

Visible repair can become a creative identity signal instead of a compromise.

Strategic Fit

LEGO brings modular play. Patagonia brings repair credibility and environmental care.

Proposal lens Make repair feel like design, not damage control.

Why This Is Worth Building

Solving the repair gap creates a product line, a service moment, and a community platform.

Selected opportunity: Repair Kit Revenue

The MVP starts with a tangible kit because it is easy to buy, test, gift, refill, and demonstrate in a workshop setting.

2. A Clear Problem Statement

Target users need a repair experience that is simple, attractive, and socially shareable.

Primary users are sustainability-aware families, young outdoor users, students, and casual repair beginners who own damaged clothing or bags but lack confidence, tools, or motivation.

Family Repair Beginner

Pain points: repair feels messy, time-consuming, and boring for children. Need: a guided activity that makes keeping old clothing feel proud and fun.

How We Validate It Is Real

  • Run 12 user interviews with families, students, and outdoor users who own damaged items.
  • Collect a 7-day repair diary: what they considered fixing, what they avoided, and why.
  • Host two concierge repair workshops and measure completion, confidence, and willingness to buy.
  • Test landing-page signups and kit preorders before scaling manufacturing.

3. Product Introduction

The MVP is a modular repair kit for jackets, backpacks, sleeves, knees, and straps.

Product vision: make repair a creative ritual that extends product life and helps people show environmental care through everyday objects.

Market Difference

How We Define MVP Success

Success means users repair voluntarily, feel proud, and want the system to continue.

70%of workshop participants complete at least one repair
40%of kit buyers scan a tutorial within 14 days
30%of pilot users say they would buy refill patches
25%of repaired items are shared or uploaded for impact tracking

Recommended Next Step

Build a small-batch MVP, run the pilot, then decide whether to expand into refill packs, seasonal drops, and repair-day partnerships.
